Working Papers

Matching Diverse Types with Axel Anderson, 2026

I study how firms adjust hiring, rather than just headcount, when minimum wages rise. Using matched employer–employee data and a border-discontinuity design across state minimum wage changes, I find that firms shift toward more experienced hires rather than reducing employment outright.

Work in Progress

Algorithmic scheduling and worker turnover with Jiho Kim

Examines whether the adoption of algorithmic shift-scheduling software affects turnover among hourly retail workers, using staggered adoption across store chains.
